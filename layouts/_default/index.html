KeePassXC is a modern, secure, and open-source password manager for people with extremely high demands of secure personal data management.

Secure by default

Everything stays encrypted. Everything stays yours.

Usernames, passwords, URLs, attachments, and notes are saved in an encrypted file that you can keep anywhere, including your own private or public cloud. Without an account, nothing is ever stored on our servers; with one, only your encrypted vaults are.

  • No ads, no trackers, no cloud required
  • Open KeePass KDBX format you can take with you
  • Hardware key and key file support

Sync and share, if you want to

Your vaults on every device, encrypted end to end

Sign in with a free KeePassXC account to sync your vaults between KeePassXC on your computers and KeePass Web in your browser, share them with family or your team, and give CI jobs and servers service accounts of their own. Everything is encrypted on your devices with your master password and a Secret Key that only you have, so our servers only ever see ciphertext.

  • Shared vaults and organizations with roles and an audit log
  • Prefer your own infrastructure? Sync with your own KeePassXC Sync Server
  • Or skip it entirely: KeePassXC works fully without an account

Certified security

Independently tested. Officially certified.

KeePassXC 2.7.9 has been awarded a Security Visa by the French National Cybersecurity Agency (ANSSI) for passing its First-level Security Certification (CSPN).

The certificate shows KeePassXC complies with the security standards set by ANSSI, and it is recognised in both France and Germany.
