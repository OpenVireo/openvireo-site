Everything you need,
wherever you are

The iPhone app reads and writes the same KeePass databases as OpenVireo on your computer, and the same encrypted vaults as the web app.

No account needed

Your KeePass files on your iPhone

Open .kdbx files from Files, iCloud Drive or any storage app, or create a new database right on your phone. Changes are saved back to the file, so it keeps working with OpenVireo and KeePassXC on your computer.

  • Passwords, notes, attachments and custom fields
  • One-time passwords, with a QR code scanner
  • A password generator for random passwords, passphrases and PINs
