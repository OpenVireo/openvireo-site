    OpenVireo Nightly Builds

    A fresh build of the develop branch, made every week. Try new features before they are released.

    Built every Monday at 03:00 UTC – Nightly release on GitHub

    Nightly builds are untested. They may have bugs that damage or lose your data. Back up your databases before opening them with a nightly build, and use the stable release for passwords you can't afford to lose.

    Nightlies are signed with the same key as releases. See Verifying Signatures.

    Staying up to date

    On macOS and Windows, a nightly build updates itself to the next week's build. To go back to stable releases, choose Stable releases under Settings → General → Update channel; the next stable release will replace your nightly. On Linux, the AppImage can be updated with AppImageUpdate.
