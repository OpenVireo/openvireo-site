You can delete your OpenVireo account yourself, from any of our apps. Deleting locks the account at once and permanently deletes it after 30 days. Until then you can sign in and restore it.

How to delete it

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  • Web: open the }}">web app, then Account → Delete account.
  • Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux): Account → Delete Account…
  • Command line: ov account delete.

Before anything is deleted, the app shows what will happen to each of your vaults, organizations, your plan and your share links. You confirm by typing your account email and your master password. You need to be online.

What is deleted

  • Your personal vaults, with every copy and earlier version we store. People you shared one with keep access until the end of the 30 days, so they have time to make a copy. Before you delete, you can also give a shared vault to one of them, and it becomes theirs.
  • Your keys and sign-in details: your encrypted keys, your email address, two-step verification and your devices. You can sign up again with the same email after the account is deleted.
  • Share links you made stop working straight away.
  • Your access to vaults others shared with you, and to organizations you belong to.

Organizations

  • If you are a member or admin, or one of several owners, you leave the organization. It keeps running.
  • If you are the only person in an organization, it is deleted with your account, with its vaults.
  • If you are the only owner and others are in it, you first make someone else an owner (an admin is easiest), or delete the organization. We never choose a new owner for you.

Billing

Your plan stops renewing as soon as you delete. There is no refund for the time left, and nothing is charged again. Plans are sold for us by Stripe through Link, so your receipts and order history stay on link.com. To delete that data too, ask Link.

Changed your mind?

Sign in within 30 days with your master password (and your Secret Key on a new device) and choose Restore account. Everything comes back except share links. A plan that hasn't reached the end of its billing period starts renewing again. We email you when you delete, 3 days before the account is deleted, and when it is gone.
