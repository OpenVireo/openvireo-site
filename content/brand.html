Use these files and rules when you write about OpenVireo, link to it, or show it in a review, article, video or app directory. You don't need to ask permission for that. For anything else, such as merchandise, co-branding or a partnership, email admin@openvireo.com first.

Logo

The logo is a padlock inside a green ring. Use the full-colour version wherever you can, and the one-colour versions only where colour isn't possible or the logo has to sit on a photo or a strong colour. Always use the files below, not a redrawing or a screenshot.

App icon

To show the iPhone or iPad app, as in an App Store badge layout or a "get the app" graphic, use the app icon (PNG, 1024 × 1024). It's the logo on a dark green square. Let the platform round its corners, and don't use it as a stand-alone logo.

Space and size

  • Clear space: keep at least a quarter of the logo's width empty on every side.
  • Minimum size: 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print. Below that, use the one-colour version, which stays legible down to 16 px.
  • Lockup: next to the name, put the logo to the left of the word "OpenVireo", with the logo about 1.5× the height of the capital O and a gap of half the logo's width.

Please don't

  • Stretch, squash, rotate or crop the logo
  • Recolour it, or swap the green for another colour
  • Add shadows, outlines, glows or other effects
  • Put the full-colour logo on a busy photo or a clashing colour
  • Change the padlock or ring, or add a badge to it
  • Use it as part of your own product's logo or name

Colour

OpenVireo is green. The logo greens set the tone, and the website and apps use a ramp of greens built from them, plus neutral greys for text and surfaces.

Vireo Green #3C7D20 Primary: buttons, links, highlights
Leaf #6AAF51 Accents, and links on dark backgrounds
Forest #1B3313 Dark surfaces: hero, bands, footer
Night #0E190A Deepest background, app icon
Logo Ring #45862B The ring in the logo
Spark #C3EA14 Logo highlight only; not for text
Mist #EEF4EB Light tinted panels
White #FFFFFF Page background, text on dark green

For text on a green background, use white on Vireo Green or darker; it meets WCAG AA contrast. Use Night on Leaf and lighter. Don't put green text on green.

Typography

OpenVireo keeps your passwords yours.

The website uses Nunito Sans, a free font under the SIL Open Font License: Black (900) for big headlines, Bold (700) for headings and Regular (400) for body text. The apps use each platform's system font (SF Pro on Apple devices, Segoe UI on Windows), so they feel at home there. In graphics where Nunito Sans isn't available, use the system font.

The name

  • Write OpenVireo: one word, capital O and V. Not "Open Vireo", "Openvireo", "OPENVIREO" or "OV".
  • Don't abbreviate it, translate it, or make it plural or possessive in product copy ("OpenVireo's" is fine in running text).
  • Product names: OpenVireo (the desktop, iPhone and iPad apps), OpenVireo Web (the web app), OpenVireo Browser (the browser extension) and the OpenVireo Sync Server. The command-line tools are written in code style: ov and openvireo-kdbx.
  • Describe it as a free, open-source password manager. Plans with extra account features are paid; the apps and local vaults are free.

Forks and KeePassXC

OpenVireo is based on KeePassXC but is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the KeePassXC Team. Don't present OpenVireo as a KeePassXC product, and don't use the KeePassXC logo for OpenVireo. Saying OpenVireo is "based on KeePassXC" or "compatible with KeePass (KDBX) files" is accurate and fine.

The source code is free software under the GPL, so you can fork and redistribute it. If you ship a modified build, give it your own name and logo, so people can tell your build from official OpenVireo releases (which are signed with our key). Saying your project is "based on OpenVireo" is welcome. Don't imply that we made or endorse it.
