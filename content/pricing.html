Questions

What happens if I cancel or go over a limit?

Nothing is locked or deleted. Your vaults keep opening and syncing; you just can't add more vaults, people or service accounts than your plan allows until you upgrade or remove some.

Can you see my passwords?

No. Vaults are encrypted on your devices before they are uploaded, on every plan. We store only encrypted files.

Can I run it all myself?

Yes. The sync server is open source; on your own server there are no plans and no limits.
